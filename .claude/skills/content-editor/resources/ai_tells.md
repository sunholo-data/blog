# AI-Tells: Phrases that Flag as AI-Generated

Writing flagged as AI-authored loses reader trust instantly, even when the ideas are good. Flag these phrases when they appear in drafts — especially in openings, transitions, and conclusions where AI patterns cluster.

## Phrases to flag

### Openings
- "In today's fast-paced world..."
- "In the ever-evolving landscape of..."
- "In recent years..."
- "As we navigate the complexities of..."
- "In an era where..."
- "Imagine a world where..."

### Transitions and filler
- "delve into" / "delve deeper"
- "it's important to note that"
- "it's worth noting"
- "moreover"
- "furthermore"
- "that being said"
- "with that in mind"
- "on the other hand" (overused)

### Hype verbs
- "unlock" (the potential of)
- "harness" (the power of)
- "leverage" (as a verb)
- "empower"
- "revolutionise" / "revolutionize"
- "transform" (without specifying how)
- "supercharge"
- "elevate"

### Hype adjectives
- "robust"
- "seamless"
- "cutting-edge"
- "state-of-the-art"
- "comprehensive"
- "holistic"
- "innovative" (without specifics)
- "groundbreaking"
- "game-changing"
- "next-generation"
- "world-class"

### Structural tells
- Every paragraph opening with a topic sentence that restates the heading
- Three-item lists everywhere (triadic structure is an LLM habit)
- Bullet points that all start with the same grammatical structure
- Ending every section with a summary sentence
- "In conclusion..." / "To sum up..."
- The "not X, but Y" formula used repeatedly
- Rhetorical questions that get immediately answered

### Closings
- "Only time will tell..."
- "The possibilities are endless..."
- "The future is bright..."
- "Stay tuned..."
- "What do you think?" (tacked on, not earned)

## How to flag

Don't just delete — propose a replacement. For example:

> "In today's fast-paced world of software development, it's important to note that parsers are leveraging AI to unlock new possibilities."

becomes

> "Parsers used to be a solved problem. Then LLMs arrived and broke the assumption that a grammar is enough."

The replacement should be **more specific**, not just shorter.

## Calibration

Some of these phrases are fine in moderation, especially if the human author uses them genuinely. The tell is *density* — when a paragraph contains three or four, that's AI pattern. Flag when density is high, not at every single instance.
